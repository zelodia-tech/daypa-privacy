Day-P.A Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short: Day-P.A is a family organiser. We keep only what the app needs to work for your household. We have no advertising, no tracking and no analytics, and we never sell your data. Children do not need an email address or a password. A parent can delete the whole account at any time from inside the app.

1. Who we are

Day-P.A ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Zelodia Velani Weenink, an individual based in Ireland, who is the data controller for the information described here.

Contact for any privacy question: info@circleofgold.ie

3. Children

  • Day-P.A is a tool for parents and carers. It is not a service directed at children to sign up to by themselves.
  • A parent creates each child's profile. We only ask for a first name (or nickname), a role and a picture. We do not ask for a child's email address, password, date of birth, location or contact details.
  • A child's phone joins using a one-time code created by a parent. The child's phone is given an anonymous sign-in with no personal details.
  • Children only see what they need: their own chores, the calendar, their own picture, and their own wish list. They cannot see shopping lists, adults' lists or other children's wish lists. Parents can mark wish list items as bought without the child seeing.
  • A parent can edit, reconnect or remove a child's profile at any time, or delete the whole account (section 8).

4. Phone calendar

If you switch on phone calendar features, Day-P.A can (a) show your phone's calendar events inside the app and (b) copy Day-P.A events into a calendar called "Day-P.A" on your phone. Reading your phone's events happens on your phone only; those events are not uploaded to our servers. You can switch this off at any time, and you can withdraw the permission in your phone's settings.

5. Who handles your data for us

We use a small number of trusted service providers ("processors") to run the app. They only handle data to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: sign-in, our database and photo storage.
  • Expo (Expo Application Services) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications to Android phones.
  • Anthropic: understanding what you said when you use the voice feature (section 6).
  • Google speech recognition on your phone: turning your voice into text (section 6).

Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where they do, the transfer relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Voice feature

  • Only adults can use it, and only when they tap the microphone.
  • Your voice is turned into text by the speech recognition service built into your phone, which on Android is provided by Google. Depending on your phone, audio may be processed on the device or sent to Google. Day-P.A does not store your audio.
  • You can read and edit the text before anything happens. When you tap to continue, the text, today's date, and the names of your family members and lists (so it can match them) are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to work out what to add. We do not send your email address or account details.
  • Nothing is added until you tick what you want and confirm.

7. Our legal basis (GDPR)

  • Providing the service you asked for (performance of a contract) for adult account data and the content you add.
  • Consent for optional features such as notifications, phone calendar access, the microphone and photos. You can withdraw consent in your phone's settings.
  • Parental responsibility and consent for children's profiles, which are created and controlled by a parent or guardian.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • Delete your account in the app: open Family, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account. If you are the owner, this permanently deletes the whole household: all profiles, chores, events, lists, meals, photos and every phone's sign-in. If you are another parent, it deletes your own account and your private lists, and shared items stay with the family. This cannot be undone.
  • If you cannot use the app, email info@circleofgold.ie from your account email address and we will help.
  • Backups kept by our providers may take a short time to clear after deletion.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy in a portable form. To use these rights, email info@circleofgold.ie. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie, or to your own national data protection authority.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each household's data is separated, and the database only returns data a signed-in person is allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect your information.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use data, for example when we add paid subscriptions, we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app where the change matters.
